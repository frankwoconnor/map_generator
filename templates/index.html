🎨 Palettes ?

What are palettes? A palette is a named sequence of colours. Streets (By Road Class) takes the darkest colour for motorways and lightest for paths. Buildings (by size/distance) maps the palette across the data range. Create palettes below, then pick them in the layer settings — or use the Apply buttons to set them and switch the layer mode automatically.

Edit, rename and delete themes below. Apply one to the streets (By Road Class) or the buildings colour modes, then Generate to persist it.
